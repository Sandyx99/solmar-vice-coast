SOLMAR — Vice Coast is a top-down 2.5D open-world crime game. Explore the city of Solmar on foot and by car, steal cars, dodge the police as your wanted level climbs, and run five story missions for Mara Reyes — from your first wheels to the Bayview Bank job and the Kingpin's Call. Traffic, pedestrians, combat, a live minimap, and a save that persists between sessions.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```
